FUJI ARCHIVE — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Fuji Archive is a mobile application for Fujifilm camera photographers to save, organize, and write film simulation recipes to their camera over USB-C. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have, and applies to users in the EU/EEA (including Norway), the United States, and elsewhere.

1. Who Is Responsible for Your Data

Fuji Archive is developed and operated by Aldo ("we"/"us"), based in Mexico. For any privacy question or to exercise any of the rights below, contact mahayo2404@outlook.com.

2. Data We Collect

Account information. Creating an account is optional and only needed for cloud backup and stats features. If you create one, we collect the email address and password you provide (the password is never stored in plain text; it's handled by our backend provider, Supabase).

Your saved recipes and libraries. If you're signed in, the recipes and libraries you save in the app (name, film simulation settings, notes, categories, and any photo you personally attach) are synced to our servers so you can view them on our companion "My Recipes" website with the same account. This data is private to your account — it is not shown to, or shared with, any other user.

Photos you add to a recipe. When you attach your own photo to a saved recipe, the app tells you explicitly at that point that the photo is being added. These photos are stored only for your own account and are not published anywhere public.

Usage statistics. If you're signed in, we record simple counters — e.g. how many recipes you've saved or shared, and how many collages you've created — to understand feature usage. These are tied to your account, not sold, and never used for advertising.

Camera data. When you connect your Fujifilm camera via USB-C, the app reads and writes recipe settings (film simulation, grain effect, color chrome, dynamic range, tone curves, sharpness, clarity, noise reduction, white balance) to a custom preset slot (C1–C7) you choose. This happens entirely on your device — camera settings are never sent to our servers unless you separately choose to save that recipe to your account.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (EU/EEA Users)

  • Performance of a contract — account creation, cloud sync of your recipes, and the companion website all exist to provide the feature you asked for.
  • Legitimate interest — aggregate usage statistics, kept to a minimum, to understand and improve the app.
  • Consent — anything optional beyond the above (e.g. a future feature you'd need to opt into) would rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Operate the app's core features (recipe storage, camera writing, collage creation)
  • Sync your own saved recipes/libraries so you can view them on the companion website
  • Show anonymized, aggregate usage counters to understand how features are used

We do not sell your data. We currently do not run any advertising or analytics SDKs in this app. If we ever introduce advertising, we will update this policy in advance and, where legally required (e.g. for EEA/UK users), ask for your consent before showing personalized ads.

5. USB Camera Access

The app communicates with your Fujifilm camera over USB-C using the standard Android USB host APIs. This is used solely to read and write recipe settings on a preset slot you select. The app does not access your photos, files, or memory card contents. All USB communication happens on-device.

6. Data Retention

If you never create an account, your recipes never leave your device and are kept only until you delete them or uninstall the app. If you have an account, your synced recipes/libraries and usage stats are kept for as long as your account is active, and deleted within 30 days of a verified account-deletion request.

7. International Data Transfers

Our backend (Supabase) stores account and synced-recipe data in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany region). If you use the app from outside the EU/EEA (e.g. the United States), your data is transferred to and stored in the EU for this purpose — it is not transferred onward outside the EU/EEA.

8. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and storage for your account, saved recipes, and usage stats. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website. GitHub may log basic technical access data (e.g. IP address) per its own privacy statement. This website itself sets no cookies and includes no trackers.

9. Data Storage & Security

Account data and any recipes you choose to save while signed in are stored on servers provided by Supabase, which encrypts data in transit (TLS) and at rest. If you never create an account, none of your recipes leave your device.

10. Your Rights

If you're in the EU/EEA (including Norway) or UK, you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; correct inaccurate data; erase your data; restrict or object to processing; receive a portable copy of your data; and withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you're a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request its deletion or correction, and opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA) — we do not sell or share personal information. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

Everyone can also: use the app entirely offline with no account, so no data ever leaves your device; delete individual recipes/photos at any time from within the app; and request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us below.

11. Children's Privacy

Fuji Archive is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the app or by updating the date above. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance.

13. Contact
